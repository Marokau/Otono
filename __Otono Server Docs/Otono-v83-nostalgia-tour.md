# My v83 nostalgia tour

Levels 1 to 200 • Full chapter draft for review

A solo-first journey built from your existing @wherelevel destinations. Choose a region, spend time there, and let its quests lead you somewhere interesting. The chapter windows organize the journey; they are not EXP rankings or boss-readiness guarantees.

## The proposed first screen

- Continue my tour — opens my current level chapter
- Browse all chapters — revisit anything
- Party activities — save for later
- Class Night Lord — existing SP and gear reference

## The journey at a glance

| Level chapter | Main stay | Optional direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–20 | Maple Island and early Victoria | Small town stories |
| 21–29 | Ant Tunnel and Perion | Jane’s hunting errand |
| 30–49 | Victoria dungeon or first Ludibrium visit | Mushroom Kingdom, Kerning Square, Omega Sector, Ariant |
| 50–69 | Orbis and El Nath | Korean Folk Town, Mu Lung, Magatia, Ellin Forest |
| 70–89 | Deeper El Nath and the Sanctuary | NLC, Neo City, underwater preview |
| 90–119 | Deep Ludibrium or deep Aqua Road | Himes, Leafre, early Temple of Time |
| 120–149 | Fourth job, Leafre and Temple progression | Papulatus and regional boss goals |
| 150–200 | Finish your chosen adventures | Expeditions when you want group play |

## How a region opens

Short introduction → Monsters & training maps / Quests & discoveries / Bosses when relevant. Mob names open their map details directly. Side names stay beside the mob, never in another folder. More details reveals one scrollable text page. GM teleport keeps its confirmation.

Chapter boundaries do not remove access to the guide. Quest caps still matter: Mushroom Kingdom and Kerning Square deserve an early look. Repeated favorites later in the tour use links back to their original entries instead of filling a new mob list with lower-level enemies.

## Ludibrium and Papulatus

Visit Mr. Bouffon in Path of Time (220050300). Begin Protect Ludibrium at level 60, then complete The Replacement Time Energy. Accept The Time Sphere; while it is active, speak to Flo in the same map for A Piece of Crack. Finish Flo’s request and The Time Sphere. Papulatus becomes available at level 80 after both prerequisites.

Mr. Bouffon explains the Ludibrium Medal and supplies the Piece of Cracked Dimension. Follow his instructions for the guards and the summoning location. Create a party with yourself as leader; the event accepts one player and has a 45-minute timer. A guide teleport goes to Deep Inside the Clocktower (220080000), followed by normal entry.

## Neo City

At level 70, speak to Han the Broker in Magatia for The Suspicious Wanderer. Follow The Wanderer’s Whereabouts 1 and 2 through Syras and Isa the Station Guide. Meet Andy at Tera Forest Time Gate, reached from Minar Forest East Border. Complete Andy the Time Traveler, then Nex the Time Guard. Return after Guardian Nex’s test to unlock Year 2021 at the Time Gate. Later destinations open through further progression.

## Temple of Time

Corba is in Leafre Station (240000110), not Leafre town. Ask to become a dragon, cross the flight map, and enter the Temple. Meet the Temple Keeper at Three Doors. Complete Path to the Past, then follow the lane quests and the intervening story errands in order. The local starts are level 90 for the introduction, 91 for the first Memory hunt, 106 for the first Regret hunt, and 121 for the first Oblivion hunt. Each later start still requires the preceding quests.

## Zakum preparations for later

The local Adobis script asks for approval from the Chief’s Residence Council before the trials. It offers the Unknown Dead Mine, Breath of Lava, and Eyes of Fire stages. Save the actual expedition for group play; the local battle event declares six players. This page should introduce the goal without asking you to learn bot parties during your solo tour.
