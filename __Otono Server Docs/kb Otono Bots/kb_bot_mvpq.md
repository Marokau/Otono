The event lasts 45 minutes, with a ten-minute treasure room after MV's defeat. Charles retains the ordinary entry/search/details choices and adds a separate BotPQ Menu for assembly and dismissal.

## Stage behavior and player guidance

Before moving, the crew explains each map's objective and human responsibilities using ordinary speech/bubbles. Pink notices mark letter readiness and accepted-stage progression. The course driver attacks real reactors, gathers Secret Letters (4032118), and brings them to the leader. Ground letters count toward the required total to avoid unnecessary extra box cycles, but natural reactor drops must still be collected during delivery. Companions do not reclaim player-dropped handoff letters. Active assigned crews protect letter drops from normal expiration while the event remains active.

The human leader picks up the delivered letters and performs the guide check. The bots escort the letter holder, gather beside the opened tunnel, announce readiness, and wait for the human to enter first. The controlled human-mode test left all 15 letters on the floor for over 2.5 minutes, with zero letters in the leader's inventory and the guide still uncleared; afterward all five companions stayed in the course beside the tunnel for 15 seconds while the leader waited. Test leaders use the same NPC interaction and portal paths. The boss driver gathers the party before normally breaking the rock that spawns MV (9400589). It uses existing combat, potion and support-skill systems. Original boss gear and treasure-room drops are reserved for the human; bots help attack treasure boxes rather than taking their rewards. The ten-second post-boss transition and ten-minute bonus timer are native event behavior.

## Movement findings

All movement uses existing player-compatible physics, ropes, learned movement skills and real portals. No instance coordinate warps, stage-property injection, granted letters or artificial boss damage are used.

The inherited central starting islands had no ordinary route to the surrounding course. After explicit approval, normal event entry and the native pit-reset script share MvpqEntry.startingPosition at (900,245), beside the existing bottom-right ladders. This applies to all parties and needs no client WZ edit. It changes the starting/reset point only: all 15 letters, the guide check, traversal, boss and reward requirements remain.

Central islands remain reachable while searching boxes. BotMvpqCourseReset uses ordinary right-arrow ground/air motion into the real pit reset only when the current work/handoff/exit goal is unreachable. It must not preempt a reachable island box merely because the guide is unreachable from there. Regressions verify the complete unbuffed Beginner route, bounded per-tick fall recovery and opening-work priority; live mixed crews recovered and finished the course. No bot invokes a reset portal remotely or snaps to the new start.

Two independently reproduced shared navigation defects were fixed during testing. Rope exits had been baked using an unknown collision group even though a live mover retains its prior ground group; graph version 79 requires the same landing for every relevant wall group. Repeated launch-window samples are memoized within a graph-build thread to keep warmup practical. Separately, navigation could attach an authored airborne arc to a nearby overhead ledge before landing, replace its collision group and turn a valid jump into a wall loop. Authored arcs now remain airborne until physics lands them, and airborne coordinate probes do not overwrite the retained ground region.

Concurrent arrivals at the course's fall-reset portals exposed cached GraalJS contexts being entered on multiple threads. PortalScriptManager protects cache creation and serializes each individual script's invocation. Unrelated scripts retain independent execution locks; native portal logic remains unchanged.
